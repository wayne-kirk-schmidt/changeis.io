How We Work

Triage. Stabilize.
Remediate. Recover. Prevent.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence — because sustainable platforms are not built by skipping steps.

01
Triage

Prioritize under uncertainty. Identify the 20% of work that eliminates 80% of the risk. Understand the system before touching it.

02
Stabilize & Fix

Contain the risk. Execute on what matters now. Build confidence through fast, visible, reversible improvements.

03
Recover & Prevent

Design for long-term stability. Reduce future risk and recurrence. Leave the team knowing how to sustain and extend what was built.
