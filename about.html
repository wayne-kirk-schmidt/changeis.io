About ChangeIS

Built on the core belief:
change should be durable.

ChangeIS was founded on the observation that most technology transformations fail not because of the technology — but because they're built to impress rather than to last. Every system we build is designed to outlast the engagement.

Founded by Wayne Kirk Schmidt, ChangeIS brings deep experience across finance, fintech, insurance, and infrastructure — from global investment banking trading floors in New York and Tokyo to independent advisory across three continents.

ChangeIS moves fluidly between architecture, build, and advisory — mixing technical, operational, and governance depth across every engagement.
